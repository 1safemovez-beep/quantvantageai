# Privacy Policy: QuantVantage AI
**Last Updated:** September 13, 2026

## 1. Information You Submit
When you enter an app name, venture details, or health metrics into QuantVantage AI, that content is used to generate the requested AI output.

## 2. AI Processing
Submitted prompts are sent to the app's AI processing provider so the requested analysis or insights can be generated.

## 3. Payments
Purchase and subscription links in this project send users to Stripe-hosted checkout pages. Payment information is collected and processed by Stripe on those pages, not by this static site.

## 4. Support Requests
If you contact support or submit a deletion request, we may use the contact details and transaction information you provide to respond to the request and locate related support records.

## 5. Data Deletion Requests
You may request deletion of support records associated with your purchase or support request by emailing support@quantvantage-ai.com with the subject line `Data Deletion Request` and including your email address or transaction reference.
