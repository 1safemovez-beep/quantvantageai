# Refund Policy: QuantVantage AI

## 1. Digital Product Policy
Due to the digital nature of our AI Market Analysis reports, which are delivered instantly upon payment, we generally do not offer refunds once a report has been generated.

## 2. Exceptional Circumstances
We may offer a full or partial refund in the following cases:
- Technical failure preventing report generation.
- Double-charging for the same report.
- Subscription cancellation requested within 24 hours of a recurring charge.

## 3. Contact
To request a refund, please email support@quantvantage-ai.com with your transaction ID.
